Our Story

Samriddhi Designer Fabrics

Samriddhi Designer Fabrics is Indore's premier destination for exclusive, high-quality fabrics and materials. Located conveniently on the 1st floor above BIBA at Janjeerwala Square, New Palasia, we bring you a curated collection of rich, luxurious fabrics that cater to every taste — from opulent bridal wear to contemporary home décor. With a rating of 4.4 out of 5 from over 234 satisfied customers, we have earned the trust and love of fabric enthusiasts across Madhya Pradesh.

At Samriddhi, we believe that the right fabric transforms a vision into a masterpiece. Our knowledgeable and courteous staff are always on hand to guide you in selecting the perfect material for your needs, whether you are designing a wedding ensemble, a festive outfit, or adorning your home. We keep our collection fresh, updated, and vast — ensuring that every visit feels like a new discovery. Open seven days a week from 11:00 AM to 9:30 PM, we make premium fabric shopping accessible and delightful for everyone.
